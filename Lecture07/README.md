This project evaluates spectral gating for audio denoising (Option B). Testing prop_decrease of 0.5 and 1.0 on a noisy mixture shows that moderate suppression (0.5) delivers a higher objective SNR of 7.37 dB and natural voice quality. In contrast, complete suppression (1.0) degrades SNR to 5.01 dB due to musical noise artifacts and vocal distortions, showing that higher suppression does not mean better speech quality. A key limitation is that spectral gating assumes stationary noise and fails to track sudden variations without creating artificial distortions.
