# Lecture 02 – Sampling and Aliasing

 ## Objective

 testing and proving the relationship between sampling frequency and aliasing

 ## Nyquist Analysis

 Nyquist frequency: 20Hz

 ## Results

 The sample aliases until the frequency becomes above 25Hz.

 ## Aliasing Discussion

 When frequency <= 25. The sample has too low frequency and it misleads the system.

 ## Engineering Recommendation

 50Hz and above. It looks close to the original signal.
